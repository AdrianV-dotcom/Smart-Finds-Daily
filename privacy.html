Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

This policy explains what information PinReady Pro Intelligence Engine may access, why it is used, how it is stored, and how you can request deletion.

1. About the application

PinReady Pro Intelligence Engine (“PinReady Pro”, “the application”, “we”, “us”) is a private affiliate marketing operations tool used to research products, create and manage Pinterest content, validate affiliate destinations, publish authorized Pins, and review performance data.

2. Information we may access

When you connect third-party services, the application may access data necessary to provide its features.

  • Pinterest: account/profile information made available through Pinterest OAuth, boards, Pins, publishing status, and supported analytics or performance metrics.
  • Amazon-related services: product identifiers, product metadata, availability, affiliate destination information, and other information made available through authorized Amazon services.
  • Keepa: product history and marketplace signals returned through an authorized Keepa API connection.
  • OpenAI: product and creative instructions may be sent to OpenAI to generate structured Pin content or images when those features are used.
  • Application data: candidates, evidence, scores, drafts, boards, queues, analytics, audit records, configuration, and related operational data.

3. Pinterest authentication

Pinterest access is handled through Pinterest’s official OAuth authorization process. PinReady Pro does not ask for or store your Pinterest password. Access is limited to the permissions you authorize.

OAuth access and refresh tokens may be stored by the application so that authorized Pinterest features can continue to work. The application is designed to protect stored OAuth tokens using encryption.

4. How information is used

Information is used only to operate and improve the application, including to:

  • sync and organize Pinterest boards and Pins;
  • publish Pins when explicitly authorized by the operator and application settings;
  • retrieve supported Pinterest analytics;
  • research and validate products and affiliate destinations;
  • generate Pinterest titles, descriptions, overlays, alt text, and creative assets;
  • run compliance, rights, destination, duplicate, and quality checks;
  • measure performance and improve future product or creative decisions;
  • maintain security, audit history, troubleshooting, and system integrity.

5. Data storage

The application is primarily designed to run as a private local or single-node system controlled by the operator. Operational data may be stored in the application’s database and configured storage. If the application is later hosted on a server, data may be stored on that server and its configured backups.

6. Data sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only as required to deliver a requested feature. These providers may include Pinterest, Amazon services, Keepa, OpenAI, and hosting or infrastructure providers used by the operator.

Each third-party service processes data according to its own terms and privacy policy.

7. Data retention

Data is retained for as long as needed to operate the application, maintain audit and performance history, satisfy legal or platform obligations, or until it is deleted by the operator. Retention may vary by data type.

8. Data deletion and revoking access

You may revoke Pinterest access through Pinterest account settings or disconnect Pinterest from the application where that feature is available. You may also request deletion of stored data by contacting the application operator.

Privacy and deletion requests can be sent to adrian.k.vonheim@live.no.

9. Security

The application uses security controls intended to protect stored credentials and operational data, including server-side secret handling, encrypted OAuth-token storage, CSRF/state validation, secure session controls, rate and request limits, managed file paths, and audit logging. No system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

PinReady Pro is not directed to children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. International processing

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the operator’s country. Their own privacy policies describe the locations and safeguards they use.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, integrations, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, data access requests, or deletion requests, contact: adrian.k.vonheim@live.no.

14. Independent service

PinReady Pro Intelligence Engine is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Pinterest, Amazon, Keepa, or OpenAI.
